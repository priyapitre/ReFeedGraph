ReFeedGraph is by Priya Pitre, Omkar Joshi, Timon Ziegenbein, and Henning Wachsmuth.

Nair et al. (2024), *Closing the Loop: Learning to Generate Writing Feedback via
Language Model Simulated Student Revisions* (PROF), EMNLP.

trained models. Conceptual attribution does not imply a runtime dependency.

frameworks. They are not official endorsements. The UADC guide identifies the
Malaysia UADC 2023 handbook as its source; preserve that attribution. See the paper
